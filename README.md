Neon Bay Cover Studio — turn your photo and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art for the fictional city of Neon Bay, with per-photo crop and positioning, sunset and neon colour grades, and PNG exports at 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080, all offline in your browser. Your photos never leave your machine, and every character, place and letterform is original: the sample art is drawn in code, the caption face is the OFL-licensed Anton, and nothing comes from Rockstar Games.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Pick a layout (Triptych, Split or Hero), a colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Night or Teal Dusk), load up to three of your own photos or a code-drawn sample illustration, crop and position each one with the sliders, write your caption, and hit Export PNG. The preview is exactly what the export draws.
